# Kanyapat Chaiphad — Resume & Portfolio

This website is designed as a part of my job application, giving recruiters and hiring teams a quick overview of who I am, what I can do and the projects I have worked on.

### What You Can Find Here

* **About Me** — Education, background, and career interests
* **Skills** — Front-end, Back-end, Database, and Software Testing
* **Projects** — Selected projects with technologies used and my contributions
* **Experience** — Internship and relevant experience
* **Resume** — Downloadable PDF version of my resume
* **Contact** — GitHub, LinkedIn and contact information

### Why I Built This

Instead of presenting my experience only through a PDF resume, I wanted to create a place where my work can be **seen, explored and experienced directly**.

This website itself is also part of my portfolio — built with **Next.js and React** to demonstrate my ability to design and develop a real web application.
